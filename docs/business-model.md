# Business Model: Independent Small-Scale Fishery Operations

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-6222`

## Customer

- local fish markets
- restaurants
- community-supported fisheries

## Offer

- catch planning
- gear-check support
- catch documentation
- quota tracking
- reporting

## Revenue

- per-catch fee
- monthly operations platform fee
- reporting package

## Trust Controls

- no gear deployment near protected-species zones without governor gate
- bycatch incidents cannot be suppressed
- catch and quota records are auditable, not editable
